Research

Applied AI research on software, knowledge graphs, and scientific metadata.

Jenifer is completing a part-time PhD in Artificial Intelligence focused on research software classification, scientific knowledge graphs, semantic alignment, and reproducible research infrastructure.

PhD focus

Classifying research software with machine learning and semantic knowledge.

The research asks how repository metadata, publication text, taxonomies, and scientific knowledge graphs can support scalable and interpretable research software classification.

The practical goal is better discovery and reuse of software that supports computational science, without treating repositories as isolated code artifacts.
