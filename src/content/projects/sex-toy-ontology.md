The Sex Toy Ontology models products, materials, anatomy, use cases, safety properties, and compatibility in a domain with inconsistent terminology. It powers the public KISS AI demo.

## Context

Product information in this domain can be fragmented, euphemistic, inconsistent, or missing details that affect safety and compatibility. A semantic model can make the information more explicit while still requiring careful language and privacy-aware product design.

## My role

I develop the ontology and use it as a foundation for the public KISS AI demo. The work combines ontology engineering, product modeling, inclusive terminology, and explainable recommendation concepts.

## Approach

The ontology represents product types, materials, anatomy, use cases, safety properties, compatibility constraints, and terminology. SHACL-style thinking helps make validation and consistency part of the model rather than an afterthought.

The project applies ontology-engineering methods to product search and information quality while accounting for inclusivity, privacy, and safety. The semantic model is intended to support clearer discovery, catalog interoperability, and explainable recommendations.
