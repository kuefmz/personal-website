The Semantic Web Science Association website is the public home of SWSA, a non-profit association supporting scholarly work in the Semantic Web and related fields.

## Context

The site needs to serve an international academic community while keeping association information, governance, news, conference responsibilities, awards, and community activities easy to find.

## My role

I worked on the public website and its technical maintenance, including repository ownership, custom-domain configuration, analytics, and content updates for the association's evolving activities.

## Work included

- Maintain the public SWSA website and domain setup.
- Improve the information structure around association activities and resources.
- Add analytics for visibility into website usage.
- Support updated ISWC and ESWC-related content as SWSA's responsibilities evolve.
- Keep the site useful for researchers, members, organizers, and the broader Semantic Web community.

## Why it matters

Academic association websites are infrastructure: they need to preserve institutional information, make responsibilities discoverable, and remain maintainable as committees, conferences, and community programs change.
