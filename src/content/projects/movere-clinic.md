Movere Clinic is a healthcare website built to make the clinic's services easier to understand and to give patients a clear path from first visit to enquiry.

## Context

Healthcare websites need to balance visual trust, clear information, mobile usability, search visibility, and careful wording. Visitors often arrive with a specific need and should be able to understand the clinic's offer without navigating a complicated site.

## My role

I worked on the website through Websiteli, covering the site structure, responsive implementation, presentation of services, SEO foundations, and the patient enquiry journey.

## Approach

- Organize clinic and treatment information around patient questions.
- Keep the visual direction calm, credible, and easy to scan.
- Make contact and enquiry paths visible without overwhelming the content.
- Build a responsive structure that works well on mobile and desktop.
- Apply technical SEO foundations without inventing medical claims.

## Result

The finished site provides a focused public presence for the clinic and demonstrates how a healthcare website can combine service information, trust signals, responsive design, and a clear conversion path.
